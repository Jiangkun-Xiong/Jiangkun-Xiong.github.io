ABOUT JIANGKUN

Medicine, with room to explore.

I am Jiangkun, a final-year medical student with research experience in the UK and a growing interest in computational methods.

05

I study Clinical Medicine at Chongqing Medical University through its joint programme with the University of Leicester. During my academic year in the UK, I completed a BSc (Hons) in Clinical Sciences with Upper Second-Class Honours (2:1) and a six-month research placement in cerebrovascular physiology and stroke.

I am currently completing my final-year clinical placement in Chongqing. I hope to deepen my quantitative and computational training while keeping clinically meaningful questions at the centre of my work.

Beyond research, I have helped develop a diagnostic-kit project, supported community mental-health fieldwork and founded a student-led youth club. These experiences shaped how I work with people and communicate complex ideas.
