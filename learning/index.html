LEARNING ROADMAP

One question, a connected learning path.

How can data help prioritise lncRNAs for further validation? Each stage below follows from a gap left by the previous one.

THE LOGIC

Start with the score. Follow its evidence.

The learning order starts with an inspectable FIS component, traces its inputs, then tests what its interpretation can support. It is a learning route through the method, rather than the full tool execution order.

    Turn ranking into a testable hypothesis

    What can a high score support, and what additional evidence is needed?

    Why this step

    Ready to move on when
    One sourced annotation example, with an alternative explanation, controls and a validation idea.

A CONDITIONAL BRANCH

When does single-cell analysis belong?

When the question needs cell-type, cell-state or within-cell expression information that an overall average cannot provide. First justify that need; then choose a suitable tutorial and dataset.
