04 / ABOUT ME

Medicine is my starting point.

I am a final-year medical student at Chongqing Medical University. Through its joint programme with the University of Leicester, I earned a BSc (Hons) in Clinical Sciences and completed a six-month research placement focused on cerebrovascular physiology and stroke.

I want to develop the quantitative skills to investigate clinically meaningful questions. Alongside research, I enjoy creating spaces where people can read, exchange ideas and learn together.
