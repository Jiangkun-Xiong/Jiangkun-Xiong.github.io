# Stroke & cerebral autoregulation

An overview of my supervised undergraduate research on directional sensitivity of dynamic cerebral autoregulation in ischaemic stroke.

**Status:** Research overview; shareable materials are being organised.

[Personal website](https://jiangkun-xiong.github.io/) · [Project background](https://jiangkun-xiong.github.io/projects/stroke.html)

## Research question

Do cerebral autoregulatory responses differ for rising and falling arterial pressure across acute and subacute ischaemic stroke?

## Context and contribution

I completed a supervised undergraduate research placement with Cerebral Haemodynamics in Ageing and Stroke Medicine (CHiASM), University of Leicester. My contribution included physiological data processing and analysis under supervision, interpretation, dissertation writing, and preparation and presentation of a conference poster.

The analysis used arterial-pressure and transcranial Doppler measurements of cerebral blood-flow velocity, with direction-specific autoregressive moving average (ARMA) modelling and step-response analysis.
