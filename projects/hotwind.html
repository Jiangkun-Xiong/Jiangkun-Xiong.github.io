Hotwind Youth Club

Building a student-led space for reading, dialogue and creative health communication.

04

From a reading salon to a youth community

I founded Hotwind in January 2024 to create a more open space for students to read and exchange ideas. According to my project records, a nine-member core team organised 30+ activities and around 20 weeks of shared reading across programme cycles.

My contribution

As founder and team lead, I helped set the direction, coordinate the team and plan activities. Reviewing early peer-support ideas helped us focus on reading, dialogue and public engagement, with a clear boundary from professional psychological services.

Explore the independent Hotwind website

The project website brings together activity records, design materials and exploratory planning. It includes reading programmes, tuberculosis-themed games, pregnancy-related health discussions and Peer Voices; each page identifies its supporting materials.
