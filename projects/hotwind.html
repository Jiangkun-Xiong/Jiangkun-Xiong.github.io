Hotwind Youth Club

Building a student-led space for reading, dialogue and creative health communication.

04

The idea

Create a student-led space for reading, discussion and cross-cultural exchange, with room for health communication and public-interest activities.

My contribution

Founded the club and led a nine-member team. Our work included more than 30 activities and a sustained online reading programme.

Selected activities

  • Peer reading and discussion sessions.
  • Cross-cultural dialogue and collaboration.
  • Interactive health communication, including a medical role-play activity on tuberculosis awareness.

What I learned

Turn an idea into a workable plan, share responsibilities, coordinate collaborators and keep a small team moving over time.
