COMMUNITY HEALTH

Growing up, supported.

Two summers of fieldwork, health communication and support for left-behind children.

03

The need

A community project supporting the mental wellbeing of left-behind children through field research, classroom activities and communication with schools and families.

My contribution

I helped coordinate the project, prepare the activity plan and presentations, deliver classroom sessions, and organise questionnaire information.

Team work & recognition

  • Team fieldwork included more than 1,000 questionnaires across the project.
  • The project was selected for a national special programme in 2024.
  • First Prize for the 2024 Chongqing Medical University summer social-practice research report.

What I learned

How to listen before proposing an intervention, organise fieldwork with a team, and translate health-related knowledge into communication that fits the audience.
