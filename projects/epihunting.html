Epihunting

An introduction to diagnostic development, from clinical needs to a team prototype.

02

The project

An undergraduate team project exploring the development of an epilepsy diagnostic kit. It introduced me to the process of connecting clinical needs with research and diagnostic development.

My contribution

As a core team member, I contributed to market research, case collection and analysis, animal experiments, and prototype-related work.

Team recognition

  • National Silver Award, China International College Students’ Innovation Competition, 2023.
  • Chongqing Gold Award, 14th Challenge Cup Entrepreneurship Competition.

What I took forward

An early interest in neurological disease and a stronger awareness that a diagnostic idea needs both biological evidence and practical clinical value.
