Why expression analysis belongs here

The FIS exercise begins after a network has become neighbour counts. Expression analysis belongs here because it helps explain the evidence used to build that network.

Describe the data before choosing a tool

  • Record source, species, gene identifiers and expression units.
  • Identify samples, conditions, batches and biological replicates.
  • Ask whether the question concerns a condition difference or a cross-sample relationship.

Keep the two questions distinct

Differential expression asks about changes between conditions. Co-expression asks about patterns across samples. A differential-expression list is not a regulatory network, and a correlation does not by itself establish causality.

Define one small output

First fill a sample sheet and matrix description, then explain a small edge table and its rule. Identify which RNA-seq, ChIP-seq or CLIP-seq evidence remains absent from the teaching example.
