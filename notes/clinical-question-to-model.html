QUESTION FRAMING

From a question to a fair comparison

A method note connecting clinical research habits with a new data setting.

N

Start with a comparison

My stroke research asked about responses to rising versus falling arterial pressure across acute and subacute phases. The useful habit is to define what is being compared before choosing a model.

State the measurement and unit

Physiological time series, gene-expression matrices and network-neighbour counts represent different things. A new method requires a new account of what rows, columns and independent observations mean.

Match the output to the claim

In the present exercise, a weighted score answers a prioritisation question within a defined synthetic input. Explaining its arithmetic is a first step toward understanding which evidence the real method requires.

Write a short question card

  • What is the question and comparison?
  • What was measured, and what counts as a replicate?
  • Which output would help, and what would it not establish?
