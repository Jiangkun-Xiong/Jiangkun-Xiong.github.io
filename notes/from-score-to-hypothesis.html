STAGE 03 / INTERPRETATION

From a score to a functional hypothesis

A planned step from numerical priority to a claim that can be examined.

N

This is a follow-on plan. No GO terms or significance claims have been assigned to the synthetic candidates.

A score is one layer of evidence

A candidate can rank highly within a particular network and weighting scheme. That does not by itself identify a function, a mechanism or a causal effect.

Ask what supports the annotation

  • Which genes and background could have been observed?
  • Are identifiers matched, and are multiple tests addressed?
  • Which associated genes support the functional description?

Draft a hypothesis and its alternative

Record the candidate, system, observed association and proposed hypothesis. Add an alternative explanation and the controls or intervention that would help distinguish them.

Completion means a defensible explanation

The intended output is one sourced example and a completed hypothesis card. The original random-network threshold and GO steps remain outside the present micro-reproduction.
